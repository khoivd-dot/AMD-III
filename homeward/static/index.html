HomewardDischarge instructions patients can follow

Turn discharge instructions into a packet the patient can follow

Homeward rewrites the clinician's text in plain language and in the patient's own language, checks every sentence against the source, and sends only the risky ones to a human. It never diagnoses or adds medical advice.

Discharge text

Use synthetic or de-identified text only. This is a demo, not a medical device.
